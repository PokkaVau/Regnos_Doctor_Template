About the Specialist

Dedicated to Child Surgical Health

Combining surgical precision, continuous international training, and empathetic care for every patient.

Distinguished Clinical Leadership

Professor Dr. S. M. Khalid Mahmud Shakil is one of Bangladesh’s leading pediatric surgeons, serving as the Professor & Head of the Division of Pediatric Surgery and Head of Neonatal Surgery at Bangladesh Shishu Hospital & Institute, Dhaka.

With over a quarter-century of surgical leadership, his expertise encompasses complex neonatal surgical emergencies, congenital abdominal anomalies, pediatric urology, and modern regenerative medicine research.

Specialized Pediatric Services

Comprehensive surgical diagnostics, neonatal interventions, and advanced treatment programs tailored specifically for newborns and growing children.

Neonatal Surgery

Emergency surgical correction of congenital gastrointestinal, esophageal, and diaphragmatic malformations in newborns.

  • Emergency Congenital Defect Corrections
  • Pyloromyotomy for Pyloric Stenosis
  • Esophageal & Diaphragmatic Repairs

Anorectal & Gastrointestinal Care

Advanced reconstructive surgeries and comprehensive long-term bowel management for complex pediatric conditions.

Core Procedures
  • ARM Reconstructive Surgery & Bowel Management
  • Integrated Hirschsprung's Disease Care
  • Intestinal Obstruction Correction

Pediatric Urology

Surgical treatment for various congenital and acquired urological conditions in infants and growing children.

Core Procedures
  • Hypospadias Repair & Reconstruction
  • Undescended Testes Surgery (Orchiopexy)
  • PUJ, VUR Care & Pediatric Circumcision

Abdominal Wall & General Surgery

Correction of abdominal defects and common pediatric surgical conditions using minimally invasive protocols.

Core Procedures
  • Inguinal & Umbilical Hernia Repair

  • Hydrocele Surgery & Appendectomy
