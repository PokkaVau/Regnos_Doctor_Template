About the Specialist

Distinguished Clinical Leadership

Professor Dr. S. M. Khalid Mahmud Shakil is one of Bangladesh’s leading pediatric surgeons, serving as the Professor & Head of the Department of Pediatric Surgery and Head of Neonatal Surgery at Bangladesh Shishu Hospital & Institute, Dhaka.

With over a quarter-century of surgical leadership, his expertise encompasses complex neonatal surgical emergencies, congenital abdominal anomalies, pediatric urology, and modern regenerative medicine research.

Clinical Treatments

Specialized Pediatric Services

Comprehensive surgical diagnostics and treatment programs tailored specifically for newborns and growing children.

Neonatal Surgery

Emergency surgical correction of congenital gastrointestinal, esophageal, and diaphragmatic malformations in newborns.

Anorectal Malformations

Advanced reconstructive surgeries and comprehensive long-term bowel management for children born with ARM.

Pediatric Urology

Surgical treatment for hypospadias, undescended testes (cryptorchidism), pelviureteric junction obstruction, and vesicoureteral reflux.

Abdominal Wall Anomalies

Correction of omphalocele, gastroschisis, pediatric hernia, hydrocele, and appendicitis with minimally invasive protocols.

Regenerative Medicine

Translational stem cell research applications and modern cell therapies for complex pediatric tissue repair challenges.

Antenatal Counseling

Comprehensive pre-birth consultations for parents expecting a child diagnosed in utero with surgical congenital conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about serial booking, consultations, fees, and preparing for your visit.

You can easily submit an appointment request using our online booking form on this page, or call the chamber serial number directly during working hours. You can also send a message via WhatsApp.
The consultation fee for a new patient visit is ৳1,000 at Bangladesh Shishu Hospital and ৳1,200 at the Dhanmondi consultation center. Follow-up visits within 30 days are charged at a discounted rate.
Bangladesh Shishu Hospital chamber is open Saturday to Thursday from 09:00 AM to 02:00 PM. The Dhanmondi evening clinic is open Sunday, Tuesday, and Thursday from 05:30 PM to 08:30 PM.
Please bring all previous doctor prescriptions, ultrasound/X-ray scans, discharge summaries (if previously hospitalized), and all laboratory blood/urine test reports.
For acute neonatal or pediatric emergencies requiring immediate intervention, patients are advised to proceed directly to the Pediatric Surgery Emergency Department at Bangladesh Shishu Hospital & Institute, Dhaka.
